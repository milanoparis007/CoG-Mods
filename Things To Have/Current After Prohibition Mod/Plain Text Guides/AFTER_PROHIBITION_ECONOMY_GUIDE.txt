AFTER PROHIBITION ECONOMY GUIDE
================================

AfterProhibitionEconomy is a standalone BepInEx plugin for After Prohibition economy stability, shop access diagnostics, business stock repair, bank/warehouse purchase classification, Dirty Cash routing classification, front/resource audits, and staged route shop-order classification.

Install location:

BepInEx\plugins\AfterProhibitionEconomy.dll

Current purpose:

- Keep economy-only behavior out of GameplayTweaks where possible.
- Repair NPC business purchase stock when installed producing modules have no player-buy offers.
- Repair NPC businesses whose installed modules are missing or do not match their business configuration.
- Classify shop buy/sell access.
- Classify bank and wholesale warehouse purchase access.
- Classify Dirty Cash routing candidates while querying AfterProhibitionCompatibility for external Dirty Cash ownership.
- Classify legal fronts, player-owned businesses, backrooms, banks, warehouses, and resource-relevant modules.
- Classify staged delivery-route shop orders for stock, cash, price, resource, and direction validity.
- Expose bridge methods so GameplayTweaks can delegate economy ownership checks while keeping fallback behavior.

What this plugin does not own:

- Delivery-route travel continuation, route movement authority, or vehicle travel.
- Route UI rendering, popup docking, retheme work, portrait fixes, or sidebar/menu visuals.
- External DLL detection or broad-unpatch protection; that belongs to AfterProhibitionCompatibility.
- Family, spouse, pregnancy, children, relationship sorting, or relationship cleanup.
- Political starter quests, bribes, judge systems, campaign systems, AI combat, or territory simulation.
- Dirty Cash external DLL patching or external money override ownership.

Startup safety:

Features.EnablePostBusinessUpdateDiagnostics defaults to false. Full-map Dirty Cash and front/resource scans can be expensive during new-game startup, so keep this disabled unless specifically debugging full-cache economy state.

Quick live test checklist:

1. Game starts without AfterProhibitionEconomy exceptions.
2. Logs show After Prohibition Economy loading before business update repair summaries.
3. GameplayTweaks economy bridge line reports source=afterprohibition-economy.
4. purchase-stock-refresh summary appears once for business-update-initial.
5. empty-business-module-repair summary appears once for business-update-initial.
6. post-business-update-diagnostics stays disabled unless you intentionally enable it.
7. Shops that previously became sell-only recover purchase stock after initial business update.
8. Banks and warehouses report bridge availability without taking route, UI, family, politics, or external-DLL ownership.
9. If AfterProhibitionEconomy is removed, GameplayTweaks fallback remains available for economy repair.

Troubleshooting:

- If GameplayTweaks reports source=gameplaytweaks-local, confirm AfterProhibitionEconomy.dll is installed and loaded.
- If purchase stock is still missing, check the purchase-stock-refresh summary for failed counts.
- If business repair logs flood startup, keep Features.EnableEmptyBusinessModuleRepairDetailLog disabled.
- If map startup stalls after enabling diagnostics, set Features.EnablePostBusinessUpdateDiagnostics=false.
- If Dirty Cash routing looks wrong, check AfterProhibitionCompatibility first; this plugin queries compatibility ownership but does not detect external DLLs itself.
