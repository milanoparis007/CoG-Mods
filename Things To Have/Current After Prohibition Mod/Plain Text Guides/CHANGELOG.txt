Major Runtime Systems
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Added and hardened multi-crew vehicle support:
- vehicles can track a driver and passengers;
- final-goal travel remembers the intended destination;
- vehicle node authority distinguishes physical position, pending target, final-goal preview, and recent arrival;
- crew conversations, enemy attacks, business visits, scopeout, and inventory access use stricter node checks.
- Fixed storage access around safehouses and owned buildings:
- safehouse resources require physical safehouse presence;
- owned-building storage can remain visible for context, but loading/unloading is disabled after the vehicle leaves;
- transfer remains allowed while a selected vehicle is actively traveling to that exact owned storage destination;
- wrong-corner preview does not count as physical presence.
- Fixed map glow and building pick cleanup during travel:
- departure-corner businesses no longer stay glowing after the player drives away;
- wrong-corner scopeout preview is rejected;
- normal business sprites are preserved while movement UI refreshes.
- Added delivery-route front expansion support:
- Collect front route steps can show a compact + toggle;
- enabled route steps are saved in GameplayTweaks save data without changing vanilla route-step serialization;
- successful paid front collection can trigger native front expansion when the route front, vehicle cash, and destination are valid;
- logs include FrontRouteExpand arming, cleanup, skip, and success details.
- Added business-owner and worker guardrails:
- gangs and troublemakers are blocked from invalid business owner/worker assignment paths;
- restricted/family-style tags are treated more carefully so AI hiring does not use people who should be reserved for gang, troublemaker, owner, or family roles.
- Added pact and gang operations systems:
- AI pacts can form, score protection needs, build WarHeat, retaliate, and coordinate attacks;
- independent gangs now have their own lighter-weight automation channel;
- pact colors, pact membership, pact reactions, and compatibility fallback options are logged at startup.
- Added crew relationship systems:
- loyalty, happiness, vacation, gang meeting, snitch exposure, odd-job, spouse search, and street-credit state are saved per crew member;
- high happiness can protect against loyalty decay;
- low morale and dangerous activity can create long-term crew risk.
- Added personal and safehouse street credit progression:
- fights, kills, deals, pact work, goon work, robbery, laundering, and smuggling-style activity can add progress;
- each full personal level awards safehouse streetcredit resources;
- boss street credit starts above zero so the player has early reputation footing.
- Added and tuned heat/snitch/cop-killing support:
- local illegal sales can raise corner heat;
- gang hostility builds WarHeat separately from police heat;
- cop assault/killing paths can create national heat, witness/snitch pressure, and retaliation pressure when enabled;
- residual cop aggro cleanup prevents stuck combat states after incidents.
