# Days Of Prohibition Beta v0.3 Guide

This guide is meant to explain the important things players need to know before playing the beta build. It is not only an install note. The mod changes skills, businesses, crew behavior, enemy pressure, witnesses, routes, maps, and several pieces of the economy.

This beta is still being tested. Some balance values may change later.

Beta v0.3 focuses on gang-war weapon stances, outfit sit-down cooling, passive AI sit-down offers, and better links between robbery/front pressure and future war heat.

**Install**

Copy this build's `BepInEx` folder into the game's `BepInEx` folder.

Copy this build's `CoG_Data` folder into the game's `CoG_Data` folder when the package includes it.

Do not install this as only a DLL update. A lot of the mod uses `CoG_Data\StreamingAssets` for businesses, modules, quests, maps, conversations, skills, buffs, and localization.

If the game loads but some new businesses, backrooms, schools, churches, or conversations do not appear, check that both `BepInEx` and `CoG_Data` were copied.

**Included DLLs**

GameplayTweaks - Main gameplay systems, routes, robbery, front pressure, witnesses, vehicle crew behavior, crew relationship systems, and compatibility glue.

AfterProhibitionAssets - Custom asset and image support.

AfterProhibitionCompatibility - Compatibility checks and fallback behavior for outside mods.

AfterProhibitionEconomy - Economy ownership and Dirty Cash related runtime support.

AfterProhibitionFamily - Family, spouse, and relationship ownership.

AfterProhibitionPolitics - Politics, legal pressure, and related ownership.

AfterProhibitionRoutes - Route decision bridge and route diagnostics.

AfterProhibitionUI - UI cleanup, popup/menu helpers, and crew UI support.

CopKilling - Cop-war cleanup and cop retaliation safety.

**Main Things This Mod Adds**

More legal and illegal backrooms.

More business types and legal front uses.

More map content such as schools, churches, banks, and train station uses.

More crew life systems such as loyalty, happiness, odd jobs, street credit, spouse/family hooks, snitch risk, and witness pressure.

More enemy outfit pressure through robberies, front closures, important business closures, retaliation, and war heat.

Heat-based weapon stances so gang wars can begin with fists or street weapons before escalating into sidearms and heavier weapons.

Remote outfit sit-downs in the boss Crew Relations tab so hostile outfits can cool war heat without physical contact.

Multi-crew vehicle behavior with drivers, passengers, route movement, and stricter storage or business access rules.

State murder cases for crew members who build up too many murder witnesses.

**War Weapon Stances**

Gang fights now read the current war heat between the two outfits before each combat.

Hands Only allows fists.

Street Weapons allows fists and melee weapons.

Sidearms allows melee weapons plus pistols and revolvers.

Open Arsenal allows the full valid vehicle weapon pool.

The stance is not a permanent agreement. It changes naturally as war heat rises or falls.

Player weapons stay selectable. If you choose a weapon above the current stance, the combat popup warns you first.

If you confirm the attack and use the prohibited weapon, the opposing outfit gains extra war heat against you and receives a relationship penalty.

AI outfits normally follow the current stance, but AI-vs-AI fights can sometimes breach one tier when heat, pressure, personality, and available vehicle weapons support it.

AI breaches use only weapons already in that vehicle. This system does not unlock weapons, add gun shops, or change weapon supply.

**Outfit Sit-Downs**

Select the boss and open Crew Relations to use outfit-level sit-down controls.

Sit-downs are remote. You do not need to physically meet the enemy boss.

Player options can include Talk, Leisure, and a liquor business deal when requirements are met.

Successful sit-downs reduce war heat in both directions and can lower the next combat stance naturally.

Enemy outfits can offer Talk or Leisure sit-downs. These offers appear in Crew Relations instead of opening a forced popup.

AI outfits cannot offer the liquor deal to the player in this release.

Failed, unavailable, declined, or canceled sit-downs should not reduce heat.

**Crew Relations Tab**

The crew relations tab is where several of the newer crew-life systems are meant to be checked.

Use this tab to keep track of loyalty, happiness, crew pressure, robbery response settings, witness and case pressure, and some relationship-style options.

Low loyalty and low happiness can become a long-term problem. A crew member who is unhappy, ignored, wounded, jailed, or constantly put in danger may become harder to keep around.

Some options may depend on the crew member, their role, current state, or whether the right relationship/system is active.

**Legal Protection**

Political Bribe costs $10,000 in clean safehouse cash and protects the whole outfit for 180 days. It delays federal arrest pressure and unlocks a $5,000 Call In Favor option during active police trouble. The favor can cool the involved precinct or end the remaining cop hostility.

Bribe Judge protects only the selected crew member. It requires local heat, costs $2,080 at Low heat, $4,160 at Medium, or $8,320 at High, prevents that member's federal arrest countdown from progressing, and adds a 25% case-dismissal chance. It is consumed after a successful dismissal.

Lawyer controls appear when the selected crew member is jailed. Add $1,000 or $10,000 to that member's retainer using clean safehouse cash.

A lawyer balance of at least $1,000, $2,000, or $3,000 adds a 10%, 20%, or 30% case-dismissal chance during eligible legal updates.

Turn Retainer Confirmed on to authorize active trial representation. A confirmed balance above $20,000 gives a separate 60% chance to mark the trial paid off.

Confirmed retainers lose $500 per jail upkeep update while below $20,000, or $1,000 while at or above $20,000. Confirmation turns off when the balance reaches zero.

**Pacts**

Pacts are relationship and war-pressure systems between outfits.

Joining or dealing with pacts can change relationships, pressure, retaliation, and war heat.

Pacts can make the city feel more connected because one outfit's problem may pull in allies, rivals, or related crews.

Killing pact members, pact bosses, or important pact leaders can create stronger reaction pressure.

Pacts are still beta systems, so the exact aggression and timing may keep changing.

**Grapevine**

The grapevine is a social information path. It is meant to help the player learn about people, opportunities, rumors, or connections without everything being found from one normal business button.

Use grapevine-style options when you are trying to find people, social leads, or hidden opportunities tied to crew and city relationships.

Some grapevine results may depend on your crew, your boss reputation, current relationships, and what type of contact you are trying to find.

**Finding A Spouse**

Some crew members can look for a spouse through the crew and relationship systems.

Spouse search is not meant to be instant every time. It can depend on the crew member, the available people in the city, and whether the target person is valid for family behavior.

Business owners, invalid family targets, and people reserved for other roles should be filtered out more carefully in the current build.

Having a spouse is mainly a long-term roleplay and crew-life system right now, but it also helps make crew members feel less disposable.

**Having A Kid**

Family systems can support future child and family behavior when the right relationship state exists.

The mod tries to clean up invalid future-child data on load so old saves do not keep broken family references.

Kids and family state are long-term systems, so do not expect them to work like a quick payout or normal scheme.

If a family or child issue appears, send a normal log first because the Family plugin and GameplayTweaks both write ownership and safety checks for this area.

**Street Credit**

Street Credit can come from crimes, fights, kills, deals, robbery pressure, laundering, smuggling-style work, pact activity, and enemy outfit pressure.

Street Credit Low - Opens early underworld respect, trades, and smaller criminal opportunities.

Street Credit Medium - Opens stronger trades, larger protection, and larger criminal opportunities.

Street Credit High - Used as a long-term reputation marker and may matter more as later systems expand.

Street credit also helps the player feel less like a brand new outfit once the city starts reacting to them.

**Trades And Diplomacy**

Other outfits can offer better trade options when your relationship, reputation, or street credit is better.

Better outfit relationships can unlock better liquor, drug, account, and favor-style opportunities.

Boss traits matter. Well-connected or aggressive outfits may behave differently than peaceful or isolationist outfits.

Truces, robberies, refusals, fronts, and business pressure can all affect the relationship between the player and an enemy outfit.

**Robbery And Enemy Pressure**

Enemy outfits can try to rob or extort the player when the player is exposed, weak, trespassing, or under pressure.

Robberies are route-based now. The enemy should try to move toward the meeting or pressure point instead of the event feeling like it came from nowhere.

Robbery prompts can be answered by paying, using a favor if available, evading, or refusing.

Refusing can cause war heat, front pressure, important business pressure, or a later attack depending on the outfit and situation.

Peaceful outfits should be less aggressive. Aggressive or stronger outfits should be more willing to push the player.

**Front Closures And Important Businesses**

Enemy outfits can pressure fronts during wars or high-tension situations.

Important businesses can also be targeted, not only fronts.

More than one enemy vehicle can matter. An outfit with several vehicles should be able to spread pressure better than a tiny outfit.

If the player defends a front and the enemy reaches the corner while aggro, an attack can happen like vanilla.

War heat helps decide how often enemy outfits try pressure again.

**Witnesses And State Murder Cases**

Murder witnesses are now more important.

A crew member with too many murder witnesses can start building a state murder case.

The case is tied to the crew member, not the whole outfit.

The player can search for witnesses, but it costs safehouse cash.

If a witness is found, the player can try to threaten or silence them.

Threatening can fail and may remove the chance to threaten that witness again.

Silencing can fail, and a failed silence can make the case more dangerous.

If all active witnesses are handled successfully, the case can reset.

Witnesses are not meant to replace normal heat. Murders can still create heat, but the witness case itself is a separate pressure system.

**Vehicles, Passengers, And Routes**

Vehicles can now care about driver and passenger state.

Passengers should not magically switch vehicles unless the route and safehouse behavior allows it.

Enemy crew members should use routes to reach pressure targets instead of teleporting the pressure result.

Safehouse access, owned-building storage, shops, and business actions are stricter now. A vehicle usually needs to actually be at the right place.

Route-in shop staging can let a vehicle prepare some actions for the committed destination, but the actual transfer should still wait for valid arrival.

**Storage Rules**

Vehicle at safehouse - Safehouse resources can be accessed normally.

Vehicle not at safehouse - Safehouse resources should not be transferable for that vehicle.

Vehicle at owned business - Owned-building storage can be accessed normally.

Vehicle traveling to that exact owned storage target - Some intended actions can remain available as route-in behavior.

Vehicle leaves the corner or targets another corner - Storage may be visible for context, but transfer buttons should not act like the vehicle is physically there.

**Legal Fronts, Banks, Schools, Churches, And Trains**

Banks can supply account, license, and permit-style content.

Schools can support school-related conversation and local support paths.

Churches can support church help, church loan, and repayment paths.

Train stations can offer bulk shipment style content.

Legal fronts now matter more because many businesses have more specific modules, upkeep, and economy hooks.

**Other Gameplay Changes To Know**

Some buildings are cheaper or retuned compared to vanilla.

Police bribes and arrest-related costs may be more expensive.

Trial and jail pressure can last longer.

Crew members level faster in some areas, but they also carry more long-term risk.

Enemy AI can recruit faster and pressure the player more.

Map generation adds more schools, churches, bank placement, business changes, and residential/business density changes.

Large liquor distributors need permits, but small bootleggers are not meant to follow every large-distributor rule.

Weapons, cars, portraits, names, and ethnic groups have been expanded from the older public mod line.

**Useful Log Notes**

Send a normal `Player.log` first when reporting bugs.

Useful markers include robbery prompts, front pressure, important business pressure, route authority, witness cases, Dirty Cash compatibility, and CopKilling cleanup.

Verbose diagnostics should only be enabled when the normal log does not give enough information.

**Current Beta Notes**

This beta is meant for testing. The main things to watch are front closures, robberies, important business closures, witness cases, route completion, and Dirty Cash/backroom compatibility.

If a public report says something is missing, first check whether you installed the whole package and not just one DLL.
