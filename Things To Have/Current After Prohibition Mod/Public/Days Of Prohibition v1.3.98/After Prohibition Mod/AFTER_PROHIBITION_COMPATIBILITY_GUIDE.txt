AFTER PROHIBITION COMPATIBILITY GUIDE
=====================================

AfterProhibitionCompatibility is a standalone BepInEx plugin for external DLL detection, compatibility classification, bridge queries, and broad-unpatch protection decisions.

Install location:

BepInEx\plugins\AfterProhibitionCompatibility.dll

Current purpose:

- Log a read-only external DLL compatibility matrix once shortly after startup.
- Detect Dirty Cash Economy and Dirty Cash Volume Fix.
- Detect 10K Button/Input and PIA UI Enhancer ownership.
- Detect external safebox/menu/sidebar/launcher ownership.
- Detect CoGCustomAssets, CustomPortraits, GangWars, TerritoryExpansion, MafiaHierarchy, ticker, cheat/menu, and optimizer overlap.
- Expose reflection-safe bridge methods for GameplayTweaks and AfterProhibitionUI.
- Classify broad-unpatch owners as protect or ignore.
- Classify Dirty Cash route-input, original-method override, and money-routing protections.
- Classify 10K Button/Input route-input, button-input, and menu/sidebar protections.

What this plugin does not own:

- Shop stock, buy/sell behavior, bank purchases, warehouse purchases, or resource generation.
- Delivery route execution, staged shop orders, travel continuation, or vehicle movement.
- Family, spouse, pregnancy, children, relationship sorting, or dead relationship cleanup.
- Political quests, bribes, judge systems, campaign systems, AI, combat, or territory behavior.
- UI drawing, retheme patches, popup docking, crew buttons, portrait clearing, or sidebar visuals.
- GameplayTweaks broad-unpatch execution. This plugin only classifies and reports safe decisions.

Quick live test checklist:

1. Game starts without compatibility plugin exceptions.
2. Logs show After Prohibition Compatibility loading before the delayed matrix.
3. Logs show one external matrix and one external-detail line.
4. Dirty Cash and 10K classifiers appear once per launch when their external DLLs are present.
5. GameplayTweaks bridge lines report source=afterprohibition-compatibility-bridge.
6. If AfterProhibitionCompatibility is removed, GameplayTweaks still falls back to local compatibility detection.
7. Route selections, dirty cash route choices, and default delivery choices remain visible.
8. No shop, bank, route, family, politics, or UI behavior is owned by this DLL.

Troubleshooting:

- If GameplayTweaks reports source=gameplaytweaks-local, confirm AfterProhibitionCompatibility.dll is installed and loaded.
- If broad-unpatch guard lines do not appear, check Features.EnableBroadUnpatchGuardLog.
- If Dirty Cash classification is missing, check Features.EnableDirtyCashClassificationLog.
- If 10K Button/Input classification is missing, check Features.EnableTenKInputClassificationLog.
- If an external DLL changes names or GUIDs, keep GameplayTweaks fallback enabled until the compatibility detector is updated.
