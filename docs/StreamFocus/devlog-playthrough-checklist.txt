DEVLOG PLAYTHROUGH CHECKLIST
============================

Goal
----

- Show the major After Prohibition changes without turning the recording into a full tutorial.
- Aim for a 20-35 minute devlog, not a full campaign run.
- Start from a save where the boss has a crew, a vehicle, nearby enemy territory, at least one pact or enemy outfit, and a few businesses/fronts already visible.
- Keep the first recording focused on what players can immediately see: custom assets, crew/vehicle controls, robbery meetings, front closures, important-business pressure, and enemy AI routes.
- Treat this as a planned devlog checklist. The devlog has not happened yet, so keep notes flexible while testing.

Before Recording
----------------

- Load a save that already has:
  - the player boss and at least one passenger-capable vehicle;
  - two or more crew members available for combat or travel;
  - at least one nearby enemy outfit;
  - at least one enemy boss known on the map;
  - a shop/business the player has bought from or sold to recently;
  - enough cash on crew to make robbery pressure possible.

- Open with the package folder only briefly:
  - show that BepInEx has the split plugins;
  - mention that the mod is no longer one giant GameplayTweaks-only system;
  - mention that the beta build now includes all expected plugin DLLs;
  - point out custom assets and the public guide/changelog files.

- Do not spend more than 2-3 minutes on install/package files.
- Do not read the full changelog on video; use it as a reference.
- Keep a save backup before testing robbery or combat.

Suggested Recording Order
-------------------------

- Start at the main menu or loaded map and show the custom asset/menu look.
- Load the prepared save.
- Show the crew/vehicle setup first because it is easy to understand visually.
- Demonstrate switching passengers and vehicle crew behavior.
- Demonstrate the attack crew conversation UI and the updated combat options.
- Move into or near enemy territory to set up route-based robbery pressure.
- Show that outfit robbery/conversation actions should be handled through the outfit boss, not random non-boss crew members.
- Trigger or explain the robbery meeting route.
- Show the boss crew relations robbery response toggle.
- Show or explain front closures and important-business pressure.
- End with enemy AI expectations: robbery meetings, front pressure, business pressure, expansion pressure, and possible coordinated attacks.

Opening Talking Points
----------------------

- "This build is the Days Of Prohibition beta v0.1 public package."
- "A lot of systems have been split out of GameplayTweaks into separate plugins."
- "The big focus of this devlog is what that means in play: route-based robbery meetings, front closures, important-business pressure, and better vehicle crew handling."
- "This is still beta, so the goal is to show the direction and what needs testing next."

Short Changelog Summary To Mention
----------------------------------

- Enemy robberies now start from a meeting route.
- Robbery prompts clean up better if closed or interrupted.
- Front closures should be more reliable.
- Non-boss enemy crew and multiple vehicles can apply pressure.
- Important businesses can be targeted through war pressure.
- Defended fronts, retaliation, truce checks, and combat timing were cleaned up.

Do not read this like patch notes on video. Use it as the quick anchor for what the devlog is trying to show.

What To Show: Split Plugins And Custom Assets
---------------------------------------------

- Show the BepInEx plugins list briefly.
- Mention these standalone systems:
  - AfterProhibitionAssets for custom images and asset manifest support;
  - AfterProhibitionCompatibility for external mod detection;
  - AfterProhibitionEconomy for shop, stock, Dirty Cash, and legal-business handling;
  - AfterProhibitionFamily for family and relationship safety;
  - AfterProhibitionPolitics for politics-owned behavior;
  - AfterProhibitionRoutes for route-owned behavior;
  - AfterProhibitionUI for UI-only cleanup and visuals.

- Explain that GameplayTweaks is still required because it owns shared state and fallback behavior.
- Show the custom menu/background or asset-driven UI piece if it is visible.
- Keep this section short. The viewer mostly needs to know the mod is more modular now.

What To Show: Vehicle Crew And Passengers
-----------------------------------------

- Put the boss and at least one crew member in a vehicle.
- Show that the vehicle can carry multiple crew members when the vehicle supports it.
- Show switching who is in the vehicle if the UI allows it in the current situation.
- Show that passengers are treated as part of the vehicle group for relevant travel/combat checks.
- Explain that vehicle authority was tightened so passenger moves should not steal control from the driver.
- If attacking a vehicle, show that confirmed passengers can be targeted or included.

Quick passenger talking points:

- "Vehicle crew is now handled more carefully."
- "The driver should control the route."
- "Passengers can matter for attacks and defenses."
- "The combat UI now tries to make the target and spread options clearer."

What To Show: Combat UI
-----------------------

- Open the attack crew conversation.
- Show the updated controls:
  - Attack Type;
  - On-Foot Crew;
  - Range;
  - Targets;
  - Spread or multi-target behavior when available.

- If the player is on foot, show how the on-foot crew count is chosen.
- If the player is in a vehicle, show the drive-by/vehicle attack setup.
- If only one crew member is attacking, mention that the UI should still expose the relevant spread/target options when available.
- Do not force a long combat unless it helps demonstrate passenger targeting.

What To Show: Robbery And Extortion Pressure
--------------------------------------------

- Move the player into enemy territory or near an enemy outfit that has local pressure.
- Explain that robberies are more like extortion pressure before or alongside other gang pressure.
- Explain that robberies can be triggered when:
  - the player crew is in enemy territory;
  - the player looks weak enough for an outfit to try pressure;
  - the player is near a hostile or opportunistic outfit;
  - the outfit personality and local situation make robbery/extortion likely.

- Mention that peaceful outfits should lean away from robberies.
- Mention that aggressive outfits are more likely to use robbery pressure and quick retaliation.
- Explain that robbery pressure now tries to begin from a meeting/contact route.
- Explain that prompts should not keep firing if the player leaves the area.
- Explain that robbery prompts are delayed to the player turn instead of firing awkwardly mid-turn.
- Show the prompt only if it naturally appears during the run.

Robbery meeting talking points:

- "The enemy should try to reach a believable contact point first."
- "If the player leaves, the prompt should not keep chasing stale context."
- "Right-clicking or closing the prompt should not leave the robbery stuck forever."
- "Robberies still matter, but they should not flood war heat by themselves."

Boss-only outfit interaction note:

- Robbery crew conversation choices should be used through the outfit boss.
- Do not demonstrate repeated robbery choices on random non-boss enemy crew members.
- If showing the system, select or talk to the outfit boss so the correct outfit-level buff/cooldown logic applies.
- Explain that this avoids infinite repeat use on ordinary enemy crew members.

What To Show: Robbery Response Toggle
-------------------------------------

- Open the boss crew relations menu.
- Show the robbery response option:
  - Robbery: Prompt means the player wants the manual prompt to show;
  - Robbery: Auto Evade means the prompt is suppressed and the crew follows the boss standing order.

- Explain that Auto Evade is for players who do not want to answer every robbery prompt manually.
- Explain that Prompt is for players who want to choose whether to pay, refuse, or use available options.
- Mention that refusal/evasion has risk; it is not meant to be a free cancel.

What To Expect From Robbery Outcomes
------------------------------------

- A robbery can resolve by payment if the robber succeeds.
- A successful refusal/evasion can still create pressure later.
- Enemy outfits can respond with:
  - important-business pressure;
  - a rare coordinated/quick attack warning;
  - continued expansion or war pressure through normal AI behavior.

- The same robber outfit should not immediately spam the same human target after resolution because pair cooldowns are longer now.
- Important-business pressure should target valid visible shops/businesses, not invisible invalid buildings.
- Forced-closed shops should block normal buy/sell interaction while closed.
- Robbery pressure should feed later conflict without stacking every offensive option at once.

What To Show: Front Closures And Important Businesses
-----------------------------------------------------

- Pick a save where the player already owns or protects a few fronts.
- Identify one front that an enemy outfit may want to close.
- Put a crew member on or near a possible defense corner.
- Advance turns only long enough to show the route behavior or explain the expected behavior.
- Show that non-boss enemy crew can be involved if it happens naturally.
- Show or explain that multiple enemy vehicles can support more pressure.
- Mention that important businesses can now close from war pressure, not only robbery.

Front closure talking points:

- "Enemies should get more time to reach the front before changing targets."
- "The target should not keep changing so fast that the player cannot defend it."
- "If the enemy reaches a defended front and is aggressive, combat can still happen."
- "If the player defends successfully, the result should be clearer."
- "Failed or defended pressure should cool down before another try."

Important business talking points:

- "Important businesses are now part of war pressure."
- "This means shops can be threatened during a war, not only after robberies."
- "The system should still avoid unreasonable spam."

What To Show: Enemy AI Expectations
-----------------------------------

- Enemy outfits should not only shoot first.
- They can use a mix of:
  - route-based robbery/extortion pressure;
  - front closures;
  - normal expansion pressure;
  - important-business pressure;
  - war heat and local conflict logic;
  - rare coordinated attack pressure.

- Larger or stronger outfits should feel more willing to pressure weaker crews or smaller gangs.
- Smaller gangs can still act locally, but should not constantly target far-away enemies.
- Enemy outfits with multiple vehicles should be able to pressure more than one target over time.
- Enemy outfits should still leave some crew for normal safehouse, business, and front protection behavior.
- Enemy pressure may not all happen on one turn; use a save where a few turns can be advanced quickly.
- If the run is quiet, explain that personality, local strength, territory, and nearby targets affect behavior.

What To Avoid Spending Too Much Time On
---------------------------------------

- Do not spend all day waiting for a perfect robbery prompt.
- Do not try to explain every plugin file.
- Do not demonstrate every family, politics, economy, and route feature in one video.
- Do not open every guide file on screen.
- Do not chase a rare coordinated attack for too long; explain it if it does not happen naturally.
- Do not spend all day waiting for a perfect front closure.
- Do not spend all day waiting for an important-business closure.
- Do not record a long silent turn-advance section. Cut or summarize if needed.

Fast Demo Flow
--------------

- 0-3 minutes: intro, package split, custom assets.
- 3-8 minutes: loaded save, crew, vehicle, passenger behavior.
- 8-13 minutes: attack crew UI, spread/targets, on-foot versus vehicle attack setup.
- 13-20 minutes: move near enemy territory, explain robbery meeting routes, show boss-only outfit interaction rule.
- 20-25 minutes: show robbery response toggle and explain prompt versus auto evade.
- 25-31 minutes: explain front closures, defended fronts, and important-business pressure.
- 31-35 minutes: advance a few turns and explain enemy AI pressure expectations.

Backup Plan If Robbery Does Not Trigger
---------------------------------------

- Explain the trigger conditions while showing the map:
  - enemy territory;
  - nearby hostile outfit;
  - weak or cash-carrying crew;
  - outfit personality and local pressure.

- Show the boss crew relations toggle anyway.
- Show the enemy outfit boss and explain that outfit-level robbery interactions should go through bosses.
- Mention that the system is balanced to avoid constant spam, so not every move produces a prompt.

Backup Plan If Front Closure Does Not Happen
--------------------------------------------

- Show the front or corner that would be worth defending.
- Explain that enemies need time to route to the target.
- Explain that non-boss crew can be used now, but it depends on outfit crew and vehicles.
- Explain that war heat, relations, outfit personality, and local pressure affect timing.
- Do not force the whole devlog to wait for it.

Backup Plan If Important-Business Closure Does Not Happen
---------------------------------------------------------

- Show an important business that would be a valid target.
- Explain that business closures can now come from war pressure.
- Explain that the system should avoid stacking too many closures too quickly.
- Mention that this is one of the beta test items.

Bug/Devlog Note
---------------

- If a bug appears during recording, keep it in if it helps explain the dev process.
- Normal public logs are cleaner now.
- First bug reports should be useful without requiring players to enable verbose diagnostics first.
- Detailed diagnostics can still be enabled later if a bug needs deeper repro evidence.
