# Ranked Schemes And Opportunity Quests

## Goal

Group the role-based side schemes, class / trait renames, and business-opportunity quests into one content pass.

## Scope

1. Mini vacations for boss and high-ranking members that require fancy suits, cost a lot, and give a small amount of street credit.
2. Accountant and boss can do local fundraiser schemes for street credit, with smarter characters giving more.
3. Underboss and boss can look for weapons and hijack trucks.
4. Underboss, boss, and right hand can sell weapons or rob with them.
5. Rename street enforcer to `Con Artist`.
6. Make larger burglaries available for `smart` and `connected`.
7. Add small quest lines that randomly give players businesses.
8. Scheme quests require boss or underboss to be intelligent or organized.

## Streaming Assets First

Most of this should begin in content:

- rank-gated convo trees
- item requirements such as fancy suits
- street-credit reward variants
- weapons-search and truck-hijack quests
- sell-weapons and robbery branches
- role / label rename content if data-driven
- burglary quest chains
- random business reward pools
- intelligent / organized requirements

## Patch Only If Needed

Use code only if the existing content systems cannot cover:

- stat-scaled street-credit rewards
- random business reward weighting
- role rename propagation in UI if the label is hard-coded
- dynamic quest availability by exact crew role rather than by tags or states

## Task Breakdown

1. Add mini-vacation actions for high rank members and boss.
2. Require fancy suits and high cost.
3. Reward small street credit.
4. Add fundraiser schemes for accountant and boss.
5. Scale street credit upward for smarter actors if the data allows it.
6. Add weapons-search and truck-hijack schemes for underboss and boss.
7. Add sell-weapons and armed-robbery actions for underboss, boss, and right hand.
8. Replace `Street Enforcer` with `Con Artist`.
9. Gate larger burglaries behind `smart` and `connected`.
10. Add random business-unlock quests.
11. Gate scheme quests behind intelligent or organized boss / underboss requirements.

## Desired Outcome

Crew rank and trait identity should matter more in moment-to-moment play, and side activities should feel like a ladder of criminal opportunity instead of isolated one-off buttons.
